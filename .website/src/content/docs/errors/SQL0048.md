title: "sqlstreams.consumer.session.reclaimed"
slug: errors/SQL0048
kind: "metric"
consequence: "counter of `{lease}`, one series per consumer session"
---

Leases this instance took over from expired workers. Nonzero means some
other instance died or stalled mid-range.

A session flow: this instance's own running total, flushed to
`__system.metrics` each flush tick and printed as `reclaimed_count` on
the [consumer stopped](/errors/SQL0041) line. It never reconciles against
the fleet-wide `sqlstreams.consumer.*` gauges.
