slug: errors/SS0093
kind: "metric"
consequence: "gauge of `ms`, one series per consumer group"
---

The mean time between an abandoned consumerFunc routine and its matching
cleared record.

The `stream` and `group` attributes identify the series. Only abandoned routines
that later returned take part; zero means the retained rows contain no matched
pair.
