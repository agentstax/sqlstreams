kind: "metric"
consequence: "gauge of `{lease}`, one series per consumer group"
---

Open cursor-range leases held by the consumer group.

The `stream` and `group` attributes identify the series. A lease remains open
while its claimed range is being processed; an expired lease stays visible
until a later claim reclaims it.
