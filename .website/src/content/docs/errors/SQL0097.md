---
title: "payload cannot be encoded as JSON"
slug: errors/SQL0097
kind: "error"
recovery: "permanent"
consequence: "an unchanged retry cannot succeed; retry machinery stops immediately"
fix: 'give the payload a shape encoding/json accepts -- the cause names what it could not encode'
---

encoding/json rejected the message: a NaN or infinite float, a channel, func
or complex field, a value that points back at itself, or a `MarshalJSON` that
returned an error. Nothing was produced, and a schedule that hit it was not
registered. Raised by `Produce`, `ProduceInTx`, the batch path, and
`Scheduler.Register`.

The cause is encoding/json's own error. It names the type or the field, never
the value: SQLStreams encodes the payload in Go before handing it to the driver
so that a failure never prints the document. The driver's own encode error
would.
