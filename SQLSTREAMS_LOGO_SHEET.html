  • Use amber against a light surface; use gold against navy. Single-color artwork is available when two colors do not fit.
  • The wordmark reuses the repository's outlined monospaced lettering. Body/UI text keeps the site's existing typography.
  • Keep the case SQLStreams. The semicolon follows the name: SQLStreams;. Never place it before the wordmark. Use the standalone symbol for favicons and avatars.
  • The dark website treatment uses the existing blue header colors. This sheet does not propose a board-layout redesign.

Size, space and deliverables

  • Initial minimums: symbol 16px; wordmark 160px wide; horizontal lockup 180px wide. Use the symbol alone below that width.
  • Clear space: at least one SQL cap-height around a standalone symbol; half a cap-height around the wordmark/lockup. Layouts here demonstrate the intended breathing room.
  • Accessible name: SQLStreams. Hide the symbol from assistive technology when adjacent text already names the product.
